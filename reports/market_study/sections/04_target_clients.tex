\chapter{Target Clients}

\section{Segment Overview}

The platform's possible buyers fall into the segments summarised in
Table~\ref{tab:client_segments}. They are not equally attractive: priority here
follows the Tunisian unit-economics logic set out in Chapter~2, not the global
market size. The two highest-priority segments are universities and
export-oriented industrial plants (for inspection), because in both the robot is
compared to something other than a local wage.

\section{Named First Customers}

Realistic first customers, by segment and concrete anchor:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Universities / research:} engineering schools with active
          robotics and AI programs -- for example ESPRIT, INSAT and the Tunisia
          Polytechnic School.
    \item \textbf{Industrial inspection:} export-oriented factories under
          contract with European clients in the Sousse, Sfax and Ben Arous
          industrial zones, plus process-industry plants (Groupe Chimique
          Tunisien, cement and food-processing).
    \item \textbf{Manufacturing (mid-term):} automotive-components Tier-1 and
          Tier-2 suppliers in the Sousse--Sfax--Tunis corridor, represented by
          the Tunisian Automotive Association.
    \item \textbf{Healthcare (secondary):} private clinics in Tunis and Sousse,
          approached with a low-cost or free pilot.
    \item \textbf{Hospitality (showcase):} international hotel chains already
          established in Tunisia, rather than independent hotels.
    \item \textbf{3PL / retail logistics:} operators serving chains such as
          Carrefour Tunisie and Monoprix, and regional e-commerce like Jumia
          (via RaaS, given thin margins).
    \item \textbf{Public institutions (security/inspection):} ministries and
          public facilities, following the precedent Enova Robotics established
          with the interior ministry.
\end{itemize}

The single most valuable early win is one credible reference in either
inspection or a university, because those buyers move quickly and a working
local deployment is the strongest sales asset for every later segment.
