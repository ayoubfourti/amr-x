\chapter*{Abbreviations and Acronyms}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abbreviations and Acronyms}

Here is a quick list of the abbreviations used in this market study.

\begin{longtable}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.22\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.68\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Abbreviation} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
\midrule
\endhead
A / S / C / N & The four platform modules -- Arm (A), Shelf-access (S), Compartment (C), and Sensors (N) \\
AMR & Autonomous Modular Robot \\
ATEX & ATmospheres EXplosibles -- the EU rules for equipment used in potentially explosive atmospheres \\
CAGR & Compound Annual Growth Rate (the average yearly growth rate of a market) \\
EU & European Union \\
EUR & Euro (currency) \\
GCC & Gulf Cooperation Council (the Gulf states, e.g. UAE and Saudi Arabia) \\
HVAC & Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning \\
LiDAR & Light Detection and Ranging (laser-based distance sensing) \\
MEA & Middle East \& Africa \\
MES & Manufacturing Execution System (factory production-tracking software) \\
OEM & Original Equipment Manufacturer \\
RaaS & Robots-as-a-Service (paying a recurring fee to use robots instead of buying them outright) \\
RFID & Radio-Frequency Identification (wireless tag scanning) \\
TAA & Tunisian Automotive Association \\
3PL & Third-Party Logistics (outsourced warehousing and distribution) \\
UAE & United Arab Emirates \\
UGV & Unmanned Ground Vehicle \\
USD & US Dollar \\
UV & Ultraviolet (used here for disinfection / sensing) \\
WMS & Warehouse Management System (the software that runs a warehouse) \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
